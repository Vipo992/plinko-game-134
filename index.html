How the PLINKO board is set up

A PLINKO board is built as a triangle of staggered pegs. Each row is slightly wider than the one above it, so the ball has to bounce left or right at every level. That sequence creates a path that is simple in theory but varied in practice, which is part of what keeps plinko reviews so focused on the board design itself.

With each bounce acting like a fresh chance event, the ball tends to collect near the centre while the outer pockets are hit less often. That pattern is the reason the paytable can place smaller values in the middle and larger values on the edges. Payouts, odds, and the house edge

PLINKO is built on probabilities, and every pocket value is tied to how likely the ball is to land there. The more likely the pocket, the lower the payout usually is.
